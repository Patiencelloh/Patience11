How I think

How I think about Customer Success

Success starts with understanding the customer.

  1. Silence is a signal

    Customers rarely leave without warning. A feature that goes unused, a drop in transactions, or a ticket left open too long can show up weeks before a customer walks away. Noticing early gives you time to find out what changed.

  2. Onboarding sets the pace

    A completed sign-up is not a successful start. Customers need clear steps, a way to pick up where they left off, and confirmation that things worked, so they reach value quickly.

  3. Value matters more than activity

    An account that looks active can still be struggling. The real question is whether the customer is using the right features to get the outcome they signed up for.

  4. Fix the cause, not the symptom

    Discounts and outreach don't hold if the real problem is a service failure that hasn't been fixed. I match each action to the verified reason, then confirm the fix worked.

  5. Feedback should lead somewhere

    Feedback scattered across calls, reviews and social media helps no one. It needs one owner, clear themes and priorities, and a loop that tells customers what changed.

  6. Success and Support work together

    Support fixes what customers report. Customer Success looks for what they haven't reported yet. Sharing what each team sees gives a fuller picture of the customer.

Case study 1Customer experience

Customer Experience Review

Sunrise Digital MFB · Sunrise Pocket app

Overview

A Lagos microfinance bank moved account opening into its app, but only 12% of people who registered became active within 30 days. Customers were frustrated by failed transfers and logouts, and their feedback was scattered across four channels with no owner.

What I did as Customer Success Manager

  • Mapped the onboarding funnel and found where customers dropped off, from BVN/NIN verification to first deposit.
  • Brought feedback from four channels into one Voice of the Customer process, with a feedback inbox, a theme dashboard and an action tracker.
  • Built a customer journey map and identified three moments of truth to fix: onboarding, first transaction and service recovery.
  • Set a phased roadmap with named owners and presented three recommendations to the Board.

Case study 2Proactive CS · SaaS

Proactive Customer Success at Scale

Okonkwo FinTech Solutions · BukkiBooks

Overview

A cloud accounting platform for Nigerian SMEs nearly lost a fashion retailer that had quietly stopped using a key feature. The account still looked active, and checking thousands of accounts by hand would not scale.

What I did as Customer Success Manager

  • Spotted that the retailer had stopped using Inventory Reconciliation for five weeks, booked a check-in call and found the cause: a new store manager had never been trained.
  • Retrained the team, shared a user guide, set a follow-up and introduced a bulk-upload tool. The customer renewed and upgraded at year end.
  • Researched how Moniepoint, Paystack and Bumpa use customer data to find accounts that need attention.
  • Designed a health score, priority tiers and automated CSM alerts (Mixpanel, Zapier, HubSpot and Slack) to catch accounts like this at scale.
